This is an original, informal spiritual-gifts reflection tool for conversation and discernment. Christians and churches differ in how they define and group spiritual gifts. Results are not a validated or definitive measure of a person's gifts and should be considered alongside Scripture, prayer, experience, observed fruit, and trusted church community.
